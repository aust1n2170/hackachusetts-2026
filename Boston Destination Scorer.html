Offload delay with a live feed: baseline + 0.35 min per point of ED occupancy above 85% + 0.4 min per boarded patient + 2 min per unit already en route. With no live feed, or one older than 5 minutes, the scorer uses that hospital's historical median offload time plus the en-route term, and flags it.

        
Diversion rule D-1: a hospital on ED diversion is skipped, except for time-critical specialty patients (major trauma, stroke with LVO signs, STEMI, major burn), who still go to the capable center. Hospitals, capabilities, rules and figures here are invented for the demo.
